# Literature search for a TCIM0 paper (8 November 2026)

Scope: side channels and countermeasures in compute-in-memory (CIM), ternary ReRAM cells, constant-weight codes,
open-PDK ReRAM chips, NN-accelerator side channels. Method: web search and web fetch only, 8 November 2026.

How to read this file:
- **[opened]**: the paper page, PDF, arXiv abstract or publisher record was fetched and read for this entry.
- **[record]**: only a bibliographic record (library page, slides, group page) was read, not the paper.
- **[search]**: seen only as a search result title; details not checked.
- **(unverified: ...)** marks a detail that could not be confirmed from a fetched page.
- Summaries come from the fetched page text through a summarizing fetch tool. CLAUDE.md warns that a summarizer once
  invented table values. **No numbers below are to be quoted in the paper until they have been read from the PDF
  itself.** That applies most to overheads, trace counts and accuracies.
- Nothing here is evidence about TCIM0. TCIM0's own results are evidence about its model, not about silicon.

## 8. Recommendation

### Strongest framing

A hardware-security paper whose central claim is an **encoding argument** with a cell that implements it:

> In a digital-mode ternary ReRAM CIM tile, logic countermeasures (fixed latency, random start row, random accumulator
> offset) close the timing and accumulator leaks, but the 2T2R ternary cell leaks the weights' sparsity through the
> array read current, which no logic can remove. A one-of-three (3T3R) cell holds exactly one LRS device per weight, so
> the number of conducting devices per row is independent of the weights by construction: the ternary analogue of
> dual-rail storage. Cost: 1.56× cell area. Everything is released as an open SKY130 design that passes the foundry
> precheck.

Keep (1), (2), (5) as a layered study around this ("what logic can fix, what it cannot"), with (6) brief. Present the open chip
as the reproducibility artifact, not as the contribution. Say plainly in the abstract that all leakage results are from
models of the design (RTL-level / behavioural current models), not silicon.

### Evidence a reviewer will ask for that is missing now

1. **Transistor-level traces of the array read** for both cells, using the SKY130 transistors and the ReRAM model, with
   **device-to-device LRS/HRS spread and transistor mismatch** (A6 found mismatch is the leak source). Show TVLA of the row
   supply current for 2T2R vs 3T3R as a function of LRS spread and ON/OFF ratio, and the spread at which 3T3R starts to leak.
2. **A real attack, not only TVLA**: recover the zero-weight count per row (and then weights) from 2T2R traces with CPA or
   template attacks, report traces needed vs. noise; show the same attack failing on 3T3R up to N traces. TVLA pass/fail
   alone is weak evidence (F14, Goodwill 2017).
3. **The rest of the read path**: with one-of-three, does each column's sense amplifier output (and the digital logic after it)
   still depend on the weight? The row current may be constant while the P, Z, N column currents and SA decisions are not.
   Analyse the column side and the digital logic downstream.
4. **Higher-order and horizontal analysis** for the accumulator offset (single additive mask), and a comparison with
   A2 (Gray-code counter) and B1 (masking) in area, energy and leakage.
5. **Baselines**: 2T2R plus a per-row compensation (dummy LRS cells programmed to complement the zero count) as an
   alternative to 3T3R; dual-rail duplication (A8); current flattening (A4). Area/energy/latency table against them.
6. **Programming**: per-pulse current profiles at transistor level with device spread; quantify what the remaining profile leak
   reveals (number of devices still switching per step?).
7. **Silicon**, eventually: even one measured TVLA on a fabricated tile would move the paper from "model" to "chip".
   Until then, every claim must say "in the model". The field is mostly pre-silicon (F10), so this is acceptable for HOST /
   TCAD, less so for TCHES or circuits venues; A11 and B1 show measured papers now exist.
8. **Threat model** stated precisely (physical access, supply-current probe bandwidth, attacker controls inputs or not).
9. Check that the 3T3R accuracy and energy cost on a real ternary network are reported (density cost 1.56× per weight).
